04 / THE DATA SCIENCE

More than a pretty chart.

HealthLens uses simple, transparent calculations to make public statistics easier to explore—without hiding the limitations.

METHOD 01

Filter the evidence

Interactive selections let users define which categories to compare. The chart redraws from a fixed, cited dataset rather than inventing new observations.

Interactive filtering
METHOD 02

Normalize the view

Switch from raw counts to each selected category's share of the selected total. This demonstrates how the choice of scale can change how a chart reads.

Proportions & aggregation
METHOD 03

Explain the caveats

Health measures are not interchangeable. Counts, rates, lifetime risk, survival and prevalence answer different questions and must be compared carefully.

Responsible interpretation
i
Why this matters

Population-level statistics help researchers and communities understand patterns, but cannot predict exactly what will happen to an individual. A higher count can reflect population size and other factors; it does not automatically mean a higher individual risk.
